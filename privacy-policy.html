My Chetele Privacy Policy

Effective: November 26, 2026

My Chetele ("the app") is published by Beibarys Yessiluly ("we"). This policy explains what the app stores, where, and why. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

Without an account

You can use the app without signing in. Your practices, goals, daily logs, league progress, Qur'an reading progress and settings then stay on your device only. We receive nothing.

With an account

When you sign in with Google (or Apple on iPhone), your data is stored on our servers (Supabase, hosted in the United States) so it syncs between your devices and powers shared boards and groups.

DataWhyWho can see it
Email, name and account id from Google or AppleSign-in and account recoveryOnly you
Practices, goals, daily logs, notes, league progressSync between your devicesOnly you
Nickname and emojiShown on shared boards and in groupsOther app users, next to your claims on public boards and in groups you join
Hatm and dua boards you create or claim, your reading progress on them, dedications you writeShared readingPublic boards: all app users. Group boards: members of that group
Group and goal-group membershipGroupsMembers of that group

Some of this data, such as religious practices, can reveal your beliefs. You choose what to record; we use it only to run the features above, and we process it on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw by deleting your account.

Other services the app contacts

  • Google / Apple for sign-in, under their own privacy policies.
  • everyayah.com streams Qur'an recitation when you press play. Like any website, it receives your IP address.

Notifications are scheduled on your device; no notification content passes through our servers.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all data linked to it in the app (Settings → My Account → Delete Account) or as described on our account deletion page. Deletion is immediate and permanent; server backups roll over within 7 days.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it by emailing yessilulybeibarys@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights under local law, and you may complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). On the server, each person can only read their own personal data; group boards are visible only to group members.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
