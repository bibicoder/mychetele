Delete your My Chetele account

You can delete your account and all data linked to it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open My Chetele and go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Without the app

Email yessilulybeibarys@gmail.com from the address on your account (or the Google/Apple account you signed in with), with the subject "Delete my account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (Google or Apple identity), email and name.
  • Your practices, goals, daily logs and league progress stored in our cloud.
  • Your nickname, group memberships, Qur'an hatm and dua board claims. Groups you own are handed to another member, or removed if you are the only member.

Data on your device is removed when you delete the account in the app, or when you uninstall it. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
